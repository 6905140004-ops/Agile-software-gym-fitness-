# Project Charter

## Project Name
Fitness & Free-Time Activity Guide Website

## Project Background
Many people struggle to find suitable gym exercises and leisure activities that match their fitness levels, interests, budget, location, and available time. Beginners often face difficulties understanding workout routines, using gym equipment, and finding nearby gyms with suitable pricing and facilities.

## Project Goal
Develop a website that provides fitness information, gym details, workout plans, and free-time activity recommendations in one platform.

## Project Objectives
- Help users discover nearby gyms.
- Provide gym prices and opening hours.
- Offer beginner-friendly workout plans.
- Explain how to use gym equipment.
- Recommend indoor and outdoor activities.
- Suggest activities based on budget, fitness level, and available time.

## Target Users
- Students
- Busy workers
- Gym beginners
- Fitness enthusiasts
- People seeking healthy leisure activities

## MVP Features
MVP Features:

* Gym Information
* Gym Location Finder
* Membership Price Comparison
* Opening Hours
* Beginner Workout Plans
* Exercise & Equipment Instructions
* Free-Time Activity Finder
* Indoor & Outdoor Activities
* Activity Search & Selection
* Activity Recommendations Based on Time, Budget & Fitness Level
